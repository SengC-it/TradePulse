architectures, the corrected E2 event predicate, accepted risk geometry, fixed
model/features, closed-candle rules, pre-lock intrabar declaration, dataset

`src/lib/research/m3-r11-round-011-plan.ts` and frozen as
`d406cbf9aea20b9755ad45f9fd343b2d206a4c03e4ff5994e5c1b4150e93b414` in the

`performance.status = NOT_GENERATED`, and the future execution source is

slippage, funding, settlement, or candidate-selection semantics. Round-010
remains invalidated and diagnostic-only. No private Binance API, automatic
trading, production change, optimizer, or sweep is part of this Plan.
